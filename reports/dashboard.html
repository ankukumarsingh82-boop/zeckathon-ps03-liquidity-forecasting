Caveats

AMFI figures are first releases. Liquidity data for Sep-2026 come from a third-party DBIE mirror to 17-Sep plus 6 official RBI releases; 5 days missing. Idle-cash spread (T-bill − WACR) was at its 0.10% floor in most months, so costs are small in ₹ and rank-sensitive to the floor. Net (not gross) category outflow is used.
